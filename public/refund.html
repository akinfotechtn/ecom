Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | Compliant with Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
Customer Satisfaction Guarantee: At AK Infotech, we strive to deliver 100% genuine, brand-new security equipment. Please review our return, refund, and cancellation terms below.

1. Return & Replacement Policy

We accept return and replacement requests within 7 calendar days from the date of parcel delivery under the following conditions:

  • Transit Damage or Defect on Arrival (DOA): If the item arrives physically damaged or defective. An unboxing video recorded at the time of opening the parcel is strongly recommended for swift claim verification.
  • Wrong Item Received: If the product received differs from your ordered invoice specification.

Conditions for Return Acceptance: The product must be unused, in original manufacturer packaging with all serial numbers, manual guides, cables, power adapters, and accessories intact.

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are non-returnable unless found defective upon delivery:

  • Products with missing serial numbers or tampered manufacturer seals.
  • Custom CCTV cable coils cut to custom lengths requested by the customer.
  • Items damaged due to physical mishandling, liquid exposure, or power surge after delivery.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Before Dispatch: Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to shipping dispatch by contacting our support team via WhatsApp (+91 9500673207) or email (akinfotechtn@gmail.com). Full refund will be processed to the original payment mode within 3-5 business days.
  • After Dispatch: Once an order is handed over to Shiprocket couriers, cancellation requests will incur courier transit charges. For COD orders, the ₹1,000 advance commitment is non-refundable if delivery is rejected at doorstep.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Once a returned item is received at our Chennai store and passes quality inspection, refunds are processed as follows:

  • Prepaid Payments (Razorpay UPI / Cards / Netbanking): Refunded directly back to the original source payment account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Returns: Refunded via IMPS / UPI transfer to the bank account details provided by the customer within 3 to 5 business days after receipt of the returned product.

5. Manufacturer Warranty & RMA Support

After the 7-day initial replacement window, all items are covered under their respective official manufacturer warranties (Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua, Maxxion, Realtime, Trueview, Qubo). AK Infotech facilitates RMA warranty repair/replacement through authorized service centers.
