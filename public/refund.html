Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

At AK Infotech, customer satisfaction and genuine hardware quality are our top priorities. We provide a transparent and straightforward return, exchange, and refund process for all our surveillance, CCTV, biometric, and networking products.

1. 7-Day Replacement & Return Window

We offer a 7-day replacement guarantee from the date of physical delivery for the following conditions:

  • Defective on Arrival (DOA): The camera, recorder, or device does not power on or function according to manufacturer technical specifications.
  • Damaged in Transit: The product package arrived visibly damaged, cracked, or crushed. (Please report within 24 hours of delivery with packaging photos).
  • Incorrect Item Received: The product delivered differs from the model or specifications ordered on our store.

2. Conditions for Return & Exchange

To qualify for a full replacement or refund:

  • The product must be in its original packaging with all included accessories, power adapters, mounting screws, manuals, and warranty cards intact.
  • The device serial number (IMEI/MAC/S/N) must match our dispatch records.

Non-Returnable Items:

  • Products with missing serial numbers or tampered manufacturer seals.
  • Custom CCTV cable coils cut to custom lengths requested by the customer.
  • Items damaged due to physical mishandling, liquid exposure, or power surge after delivery.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Before Dispatch: Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to shipping dispatch by contacting our support team via WhatsApp (+91 9500673207) or email (admin@akinfotechcctv.in / akinfotechtn@gmail.com). Full refund will be processed to the original payment mode within 3-5 business days.
  • After Dispatch: Once an order is handed over to Shiprocket couriers, cancellation requests will incur courier transit charges. For COD orders, the ₹1,000 advance commitment is non-refundable if delivery is rejected at doorstep.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Once a returned item is received at our Chennai store and passes quality inspection, refunds are processed as follows:

  • Prepaid Payments (Razorpay UPI / Cards / Netbanking): Refunded directly back to the original source payment account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Returns: Refunded via IMPS / UPI transfer to the bank account details provided by the customer within 3 to 5 business days after receipt of the returned product.

5. Manufacturer Warranty & RMA Support

After the 7-day initial replacement window, all items are covered under their respective official manufacturer warranties (Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua, Maxxion, Realtime, Trueview, Qubo). AK Infotech facilitates RMA warranty repair/replacement through authorized service centers.
