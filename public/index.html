Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing CCTV cameras, Cash on Delivery (COD), 3-day replacement guarantee, and GST invoices.

💵 Does AK Infotech offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes! We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across all Indian states. During online checkout, pay a small advance commitment to book your order, and pay the remaining balance comfortably at your doorstep when the delivery partner arrives.

🔄 What is the Return & Replacement Policy?

We offer a 3-Day Replacement Window from the date of physical delivery. Replacements are accepted strictly if the package arrives damaged in transit or if a different product from what was ordered was delivered.

🚚 How long does shipping take across India?

Orders are dispatched within 24 business hours from our Chennai hub via Shiprocket Express and leading parcel couriers. Delivery timeline is typically 3 to 7 business days depending on your location.

📜 Can I get a GST invoice for Input Tax Credit (ITC)?

Yes! We provide official GST tax invoices for all orders (GSTIN: 33CPAPA9563H1Z1). Enter your company name and GSTIN during checkout to claim your 18% B2B Input Tax Credit.
